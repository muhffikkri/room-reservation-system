### Reservation Availability — *Refactor* (branch `refactor/reservation-availability`)
- **Modul `App\Services\ReservationAvailability`** menjadi satu-satunya pemilik keputusan ketersediaan slot:
  - Konstanta: jam operasional 07:00–20:00, interval 30 menit (26 slot/hari), maks 8 slot (240 menit), lead time 60 menit, format kanonik `H:i`.
  - Predikat & pesan error: `isFacilityBookable`, `leadTimeCutoff`/`isWithinLeadTime`, `pendingQuotaError`, `hasApprovedOverlap`/`hasBlockingOverlap`, `slotTimeErrors`/`isValidSlot`.
  - Query: `approvedForDay($facilityId, $dayStart, $dayEnd)`.
  - Proyeksi: `publicScheduleSlots` (jadwal publik — tanpa lead time) dan `bookingSlots` (form reservasi — lead time 60 menit membatasi pemilihan), plus `dayStart`/`dayEnd`/`slotsForDay`/`timeOptions`.
- **Adapters**: `HomeController` (landing → `bookingSlots`), `FacilityController` (jadwal publik → `publicScheduleSlots`), `ReservationController` (`timeOptions` + `maxDurationSlots` ke view), `reservation-form.js` membaca `data-max-duration-slots`; konstanta/helper jam operasional duplikat dihapus dari controller; inline script di `reservasi/create` dipindah ke file JS.
- **Internalize validasi**: `ReservationService::create()` memverifikasi slot/lead time/kuota/overlap/kelayakan di dalam transaksi via modul (`assertSlotShape`, `assertAvailability`); `approve()` memakai `hasBlockingOverlap` (409 `ConflictHttpException` saat bentrok). Kelas `App\Rules\*` (5 file: `SlotTimeValid`, `BookingLeadTime`, `FacilityBookable`, `PendingQuota`, `NoApprovedOverlap`) dihapus.
- **Format waktu**: grid slot & opsi waktu lapis tampilan diseragamkan ke `H:i` (`07:00 - 07:30`, bukan `07.00 - 07.30`).
- **Tests**: `ReservationSlotTest` (unit — interface modul), `ReservationSlotDepthTest` (feature — kriteria tunggal + pengecualian `ignoreId`), `ReservationAvailabilityProjectionTest` (kesepakatan fakta antar proyeksi + lead time hanya membatasi booking), update `ReservationApprovalTest` (`isValidSlot`) & `LandingPageTest` (`07:00 - 07:30`).
- **Docs**: spesifikasi §3/§7.1/§7.3/BR-3 (30→60 menit kanonik)/§14.1; `CONTEXT.md`; `README.md`; `docs/feature-checklist.md`; `CHANGELOG.md`; snapshot 2026-09-23.
